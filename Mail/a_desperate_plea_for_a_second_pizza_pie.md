# A Desperate Plea for a Second Pizza Pie

My Dear Mr. Sam Bridges,

It is I, Peter Englert, the world's foremost connoisseur of the delicious **pizzas** you so faithfully deliver. Though my impudence would shame the very devil himself, I regret I must entreat you to bring us another of these delectable dishes, this time from **South Knot City.**

I am expecting company—a sweet couple of whom I am intensely fond. They insisted that they be allowed to take the air, but with the wilderness being so very dangerous, I refused to entertain the thought. Alas, while we will be remaining indoors, I am incapable of preparing anything but the most base and disagreeable cuisine, and so I must place myself at your mercy yet again, lest the poor dears starve.

While I have already, I am very much aware, gone far beyond the bounds of common decency in making this request, I must compound my sins with a stipulation: the pizza in question must be topped with the bacon of the incomparable Ibérico pig.
