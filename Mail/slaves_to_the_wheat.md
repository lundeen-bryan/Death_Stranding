# Slaves to the Wheat?

**Sender:** The Timefall Farmer  
**Location:** S37-52  
**Date & Time:** 09/14 22:26

Dear Sam,

Seems we've grown more wheat than we can harvest! More than a bot could handle, even!

**Say, have you been making use of that new odradek feature we shared with you?** A friend of ours developed it especially for us so that we could analyse the soil and choose the best spots for growing wheat. Ours got lost in the terrorist attack on Middle Knot City, though... **Still, we can definitely vouch for how useful it was. Be sure to give it a try if you haven't already!**

Anyhow, we've been doing some light reading to try and figure out what to do with our bumper crop, and it turns out the history of wheat is really quite fascinating. Did you know it was originally a strain of grass native to the Middle East? That's right. But it wasn't until our ancestors began to cultivate it that it really came into its own. It turned hunters into farmers overnight, changing the evolution of mankind. Homo sapiens settled down in one place and stockpiled food rather than chasing it all over creation, and with stability came society, culture, religion—all courtesy of little old wheat!

But it wasn't a one-way street. Oh no, wheat made out like a bandit. That obscure Middle-Eastern grass wound up spreading to all four corners of the globe. You could even say it was wheat that used us, and not the other way around! In fact, some scholars say just that—that we became slaves to wheat. What do you think about that, hmm? Are we about to become slaves all over again? I sure hope not!
