**Sender:** George Baton

Hey, Sam. Sounds like you’re still showing us all how it’s done. I’ve been making more and more deliveries myself, though, I'll have you know! The oxy really helps me to cope with the fear, so thanks for that. But don't go thinking I’ve got ice in my veins or anything. I still panic easy, and still drop plenty of cargo when I do...

Doubt I'm the only one, though. I've found plenty of stuff that some other porters must've lost on a run. I try to do the right thing—take what dropped cargo I can to the nearest postbox, and leave weapons and gear in share lockers. Won't lie—sometimes I feel like saying fuck it and letting it lie, but what goes around comes around, right? I help someone recover lost cargo, someone'll do the same for me. That’s the essence of the ==Bridge Link Grade.==

Most of us will never be legends like you. No shame in that. But if we work together and make connections... Well, we can at least make the world a better place. And that’s where Bridge Links come in. ==Together, we can accomplish so much more than we ever could on our own. A good Bridge Link Grade will only make it all work better.==
