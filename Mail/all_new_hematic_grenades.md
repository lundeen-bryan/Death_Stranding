# All-New Hematic Grenades!

**Sender:** The Craftsman  
**Location:** S23-21  
**Date & Time:** 09/13 06:04

How goes the struggle, Sam? **Use any of those grenades I gave you yet?** I’ve been looking into mass producing them, actually. Took a gander under the hood, so to speak, and saw that they’re fitted with a regulator that limits the amount of blood they drain from your body when you’re not feeling your best. Good old Mama, always looking out.

That said, where you’re headed the BTs are only gonna get tougher and meaner. So I took the liberty of making a few modifications. **They’re way more powerful now, but they'll also use a lot more of your blood, which'll increase the risk of anemia.** Mama might object, but let's just say I have different ideas about how to help people look out for themselves.

Anyway, they’re just prototypes, so you can't print yourself replacements yet. But they're yours for the using, so go ahead and give one a try!
