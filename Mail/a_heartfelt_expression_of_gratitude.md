# A Heartfelt Expression of Gratitude

**Sender:** Peter Englert  
**Location:** [[Locations/peter_englert|S23-84 — Peter Englert]]  
**Date & Time:** 09/19 17:24

My Dear Mr. Bridges,

I must applaud you on a job exquisitely executed. I am given to understand that the pizza in question arrived in impeccable condition. Marvelous! Alas, I must also apologize on behalf of my sister—she was asleep when you arrived, you see. As for myself, I was out and about attending to private matters that demanded my utmost attention, but I am on my way home as I write. I would have very much preferred to thank you face to face, but on this occasion, you will have to forgive me.
