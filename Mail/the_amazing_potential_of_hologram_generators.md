# The Amazing Potential of Hologram Generators

**Sender:** The Film Director  
**Location:** S23-71  
**Date & Time:** 09/14 22:26

I'm grateful to you, Sam. It really was you, every time.

I've got to say, joining the UCA's opened up a real treasure trove of data—but it seems I'm not the only one who's got some new toys to play with. **Have you given your odradek's new hologram generator a try?** It's pretty amazing. Not only do the 'grams look just like real rocks, they don't get picked up by MULE scanners either. You can even use them for concealment! It's not unlike the technology and techniques we once used to make old movies. Light and shade, optical illusions—but the level of realism is something else.

I'll let you in on a little secret. I used a hologram generator to sneak into a MULE encampment and shoot some footage. They had no idea I was there! It's amazing what kind of shots you can get when no one knows you're filming. In fact, I've decided I'm going to use the same technique to shoot a whole movie. I'm going to call it "The Great Deliverer". I've already got you down for the starring role, Sam. You won't disappoint me, will you?
