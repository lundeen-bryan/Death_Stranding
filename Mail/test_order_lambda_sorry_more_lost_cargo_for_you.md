**Sender:** T Southerland  
**Location:** Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City

I hate to say it, Sam, Half-Life lover that you are, but there's another piece of cargo I need you to track down for me—a cube. I wouldn't ask if it wasn't very, very important. So, could you do the honors? You've got to put your own wellbeing first though, of course. So if you become light-headed from thirst, feel free to pass out. As long as I get my cube back eventually, I'm not going to complain.

Repetition is an indispensable component for progress in this world, which is why no one should ever criticize you—or anyone—for taking a much-needed break. As a matter of fact, I'm taking a break now. Not that I have much choice in the matter. I'm not quite myself without the cube, you see.

Anyway, back to business: to find the cube, **make your way to the Craftsman's shelter and then head west-northwest.** You'll come across a **ruined factory, near the side of a highway,** and the cube should be in there. Once you find it, take it to the **distribution center south of Lake Knot City.** I'm counting on you, Sam. Counting on you to give me a new lease on life!
