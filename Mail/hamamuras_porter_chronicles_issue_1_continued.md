# Hamamura's Porter Chronicles: Issue 1 - Continued

"'What are you doing...'
'Folks'll be real grateful for your sacrifice...'
'Ahh, quit your bitching, come on!'

These are some few of the words Sam Bridges has been overheard uttering while faithfully carrying out his duties as a porter. Endearing, is it not?

Too many porters succumb to the strain of their chosen profession and become unnaturally obsessed with their mission. These are the poor souls who compulsively steal cargo, the MULEs; the madmen who revel in wanton destruction, the Demens. Is it the stress of one too many poor evaluations which drive them over the brink? If so, then perhaps they should taken a page from the book of Sam Bridges, for there is nothing man cannot accomplish by the sweat of his brow and through sheer determination.

Should we be surprised by Sam's feats? Those of us who believe in nominative determinism would say no, for we have always known that Bridges would excel at connecting people. We have no doubt that Sam is the future of logistics, and the future of America. Good luck to you, Sam!

Be sure to check out Hamamura's Porter Chronicles for your monthly dose of porter news!"
