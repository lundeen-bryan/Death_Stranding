# A Humble Request for Pizza

**Sender:** Peter Englert  
**Location:** S23-84  
**Date & Time:** 09/12 21:45

My Dear Mr. Sam Bridges,

It is an honor to make your acquaintance. Allow me to introduce myself—I am Peter Englert. After hearing so very much about you—all good, I hasten to add—I simply could not resist the urge to initiate a correspondence. My request is of such great import that I dare entrust it only to "the Man Who Delivers."

My sister, bless her heart, has been feeling under the weather, and I fear there is only one thing that might restore the color to her cheeks: ==Pizza.== I wonder if you might be so kind as to collect some from ==Lake Knot City== and deliver it to our humble abode? I am more than aware of the gravity and urgency of your westward journey, but if you could find it in your heart to squeeze us in, I would be ever so grateful. Oh, and my dear sister is something of a ham-lover. Please bear that in mind if you decide to oblige me.
