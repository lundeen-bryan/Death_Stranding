# **Come Roar Around the Racetrack of Your Dreams!**

**Sender:** Thomas Southerland  
**Location:** Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City  
**Date & Time:** 09/14 22:04

Hey, Sam. How are your deliveries going?

Listen, I've got something important to tell you—and you'll want to hear this, I promise.

Ever since I came to this distro center, I've been hearing rumors about a "dream racetrack". Sounds like there were plans to build one near the Timefall Farmers' place, back in the day. You know where that is, right? Anyway, it seems it was the founder of Fragile Express who had the idea in the first place. Yep. Turns out Fragile's old man was crazy for motor racing. Who knew?

Anyway, the idea was that the racetrack would attract crowds from Lake Knot, South Knot...all over. Some of local preppers would've come along too, no doubt. Would've been quite a sight. Man, just thinking about it gives me goosebumps, know what I mean?

Seems like they made good progress at first, too. Everybody worked together, and they managed to get the racetrack terminal set up in double-quick time. 'Course, then the goddam terrorists had to get involved and ruin everything, and the racetrack of everyone's dreams never became a reality.

...But maybe that's about to change, because we managed to get our hands on the original design data! And what's more, it seems like it's still good to go! That's right—**just donate the necessary materials like you would to any other structure, and your racetrack dreams will come true!** Man, I wish I could be the first to burn that baby up...but they need me here, so that's that.

Still, if there's one guy I'd be happy to see take to the track in my place, it's you. The Great Deliverer himself! Oh, and you won't have to trundle around the racetrack in one of your trucks, either. Nope, it sounds like a sleek little number known as the Roadster **will become available for fabrication if your results are good enough!**

But I'm getting ahead of myself... The first thing you need to do is **find the racetrack terminal near the Timefall Farmers' place, and donate the necessary materials!**

Good luck, Sam. I'm looking forward to seeing you race into the sunset and into a bright new future!
