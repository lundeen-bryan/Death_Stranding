Hey Sam,

Can't tell you how good it was to see you coming over the water on that boat. First time we've welcomed so much as a turd in, what, must be two years now. Sorry, don't mean to sound like I'm not grateful. I am—more than you know. Every single person here was waiting for someone like you to come along. And there'll be more waiting west of here. A lot more. You'd best go find them.
