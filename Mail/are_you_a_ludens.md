# Are You a Ludens?

**Sender:** The Ludens Fan  
**Location:** S02-01  
**Date & Time:** 09/12 07:24

I had this crazy idea, Sam. What if you're the perfect example of Homo ludens? Think about it. You're with Bridges, but you're not beholden to them. You're the only one who can expand the network, so they need you as much as you need them, if not more. Taken all together, you're pretty much free to do whatever you want, including coming way out here to visit me on a whim.

Maybe what you do isn't "play" in the strictest sense of the word—doesn't help you blow off steam, I expect—but you go about your business as you like, and your actions have a direct effect on our culture and our world. I'd say that's the sort of life any aspiring Homo ludens would envy, don't you?
