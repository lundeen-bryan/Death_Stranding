# Footsteps, the Original Musical Beat

How's it hanging, Sammy boy?

So, like, my parents were musicians and researchers, right? Well, when I was little, my mom taught me something that stuck with me. She said that the first music humans ever had was the sound of footsteps. Tempo, volume, intensity—all that shit came from cave men or whatever mimicking the sounds their feet made when they walked. Figures, yeah? You know how you can't help but bob your head to the beat when you hear a tune? How, like, you can imagine all kinds of sights and sounds, and your soul just...sings? It's all part of it, man.

Oh, and that baby you got strapped to your chest? The sound of your footsteps is totally music to the little guy's ears for sure. You gotta whistle and hum and stuff to get him to chill when he's freaking out too, right? There you go. Music—it's totally primal.

Walking on two legs made us human in the first place, and then our two legs go and give us music, too. It's pretty sweet when you think about it. And I'll bet it works the other way, too—no more music, no more humans. Whoa, that's pretty deep!
