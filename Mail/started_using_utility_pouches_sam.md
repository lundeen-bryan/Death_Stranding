# Started Using Utility Pouches, Sam?

**Sender:** Viktor Frank  
**Location:** Port Knot City  
**Date & Time:** 09/14 20:38

Sam, my man!

Lotta love for you here in Port Knot City! Everyone's talking about how you hooked us up to the network and took out that BT. Makes me feel a little better about how Igor went out, knowing that he was in the company of a real hero like yourself.

By the way, I've been wondering... **You been using the utility pouches? They make it real easy to carry an extra blood bag or two—and you ought to know better than anyone how much BTs hate the stuff.** In fact, if you've got a bag of red on you, I'd strongly recommend popping it in a pouch. Give it a try, at least. I mean, what have you have to lose?

Folks used to use all kinds of pouches, you know. Haven't seen much variety for a while now, but back in the day porters would rock customized backpacks decked out with a dozen or more specialized pouches. Who knows, if you're lucky you might even find someone to design one just for you.

Could be the beginning of a "virtuous" circle, if you ask me. The more you succeed in bringing our people together, the more likely you'll turn up some of these old pouch designs, which in turn ought to help you in your work to get others onboard. Good luck!
