# Likes - Purpose and Rewards

**Likes are progression points and appreciation.** Accumulating them develops Sam's porter abilities and relationships. You do not spend them in a shop. This guide concerns the first game and its Director's Cut.

## What do more Likes reward?

| Progress system | What contributes | What you gain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Porter grades | Delivery evaluations and cooperative activities | Improvements to Sam's abilities and online cooperation features |
| Facility connection stars | Likes earned from that particular recipient | Facility-specific equipment plans, upgrades, materials, bandwidth, and other rewards |
| BB connection | Likes from BB | A stronger bond; BB becomes less easily distressed |

The source matters: online Likes raise Bridge Link; delivering to a prepper builds that recipient's connection. A large overall total does not substitute for earning a particular facility's stars. Online Likes accumulated between deliveries appear in the next delivery evaluation. [PowerPyx: Likes and their sources](https://www.powerpyx.com/death-stranding-how-to-get-likes/)

## Sam's five porter grades

Your overall **porter grade is the sum of the five category levels**. Likes contribute to the relevant categories automatically. Category milestones, commonly at ten-level intervals, provide bonuses. For example, Delivery Volume grade 10 raises the initial carrying limit from 120 kg to 125 kg. [GameFAQs: Likes and Porter Grade](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps4/184428-death-stranding/faqs/78057/likes-and-porter-grade)

| Category | How to work on it | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery Volume | Large loads and quantity-focused orders | Greater carrying capacity |
| Cargo Condition | Deliver fragile cargo with little damage | Better balance and resistance to losing consciousness |
| Delivery Time | Complete timed orders quickly | Less fatigue; the in-game mail also describes improved stamina and longer breath-holding |
| Miscellaneous | Recovery work, lost cargo, and other services | Increased Likes earned |
| Bridge Link | Help other players through shared cargo, structures, and contributions | Strand Contracts and other cooperation features, including material requests and increased ability to give Likes |

Sources: [Gamepressure: Porter Grade rewards](https://www.gamepressure.com/death-stranding/what-is-the-porter-grade/z0cc9a), [[Mail/delivery_time_sam_people_get_tired_of_waiting|Thomas Southerland's Delivery Time explanation]], and [[Mail/you_ve_got_to_go_the_extra_mile|William Lake's Miscellaneous Grade explanation]].

**For my notes:** The **grade** property means overall numeric porter grade, such as the previously reported 171. An **S rank** evaluates a single delivery. Facility stars are a separate relationship measure. These numbers describe different things.

## Facility rewards

Deliver **to the facility whose rewards you want**: the destination awards the delivery Likes, rather than the place where you accepted the order.

Raising its connection can unlock fabrication plans and improved equipment, increase available materials and chiral bandwidth, and provide mail or interviews. The reward schedule depends on the facility; there is no universal item awarded for each fixed total of Likes. [PowerPyx: facility Likes and rewards](https://www.powerpyx.com/death-stranding-how-to-get-likes/)

Check individual [[Locations/film_director|location reward notes]] for specific unlocks. Story/order rewards also exist: an item tied to completing a particular order should not be confused with a reward for accumulating Likes.

## How to use this when choosing deliveries

- For equipment, prioritize deliveries to the relevant facility.
- For carrying capacity, choose quantity-focused orders.
- For stamina, choose timed orders; rushing an ordinary order is less effective for developing Delivery Time.
- Return lost cargo in good condition to its intended destination. See [[Manual/earn_likes_by_delivering_lost_property|Earn Likes by Delivering Lost Property]].
- Keep helping other players for Bridge Link progression.

These are practical recommendations based on the systems above. A balanced set of category levels gives broader benefits than concentrating only on one source of Likes.
