# Hematic Grenades

Anti-BT grenades introduced during the preparations for Order No. 14.

## How do I get them?

1. At the delivery terminal in **Capital Knot City**, open **Orders for Sam** and accept **Order No. 14: Aid Package Delivery: Port Knot City**.
2. Follow the briefing introducing Blood Bags and Hematic Grenades, then check **Fabricate Equipment** to make grenades for the journey.
3. After Mama's explanation and the new-order notification, check the Capital Knot City terminal again for **Order No. 15: Equipment Trial: Hematic Grenade**. This is the associated trial order.

The unlock occurs when taking Order No. 14; you do not need to finish the journey to Port Knot City first. If you have only completed the cryptobiote delivery, accepting No. 14 is the next step to check.
