# Truck: Long Range (Lv. 1)

## In-game description

Can carry large amounts of cargo, which will be protected from the effects of both timefall and MULE attacks.
This truck unfortunately shares the limitations of the base model when it comes to rough ground, and needs to be driven on flat surfaces or roads in order to show its true worth.
Also, be aware that **the battery will drain very quickly when the truck is in water.**

The battery will gradually recharge in sunny or cloudy weather.
