# Gravity Gloves

## Confirmed by my mail

[[lambda_you_re_the_real_deal|λ You're the Real Deal!]] confirms that gravity gloves are now **available for fabrication**, can collect cargo within about **15 meters**, and are **battery operated**. Fabricating and equipping a pair have not yet been reported.

## Uses

- **Gather scattered cargo from a distance.** For my MULE-camp material runs, this should reduce the walking needed to collect dropped containers. This is a practical application of the mail's stated ability.
- **Collect chiral crystals, cryptobiotes, and memory chips remotely.** These additional pickups are documented in the [Gravity Gloves reference](https://deathstranding.fandom.com/wiki/Gravity_Gloves) and reported in [Reddit: Gravity gloves will even pick up Chiral crystals!](https://www.reddit.com/r/DeathStranding/comments/1hosqg6/).
- **Collect cryptobiotes without getting close enough to scatter them.** A useful technique reported by players in [Reddit: Gravity gloves = extra cryptobiot](https://www.reddit.com/r/DeathStranding/comments/1brjd8x/).

Fabricate and equip the gloves before trying them, and watch the battery. The mail does not state a carrying-capacity increase; the benefit described is remote pickup.
