# Container Repair Spray

## Description

A spray that repairs damaged cargo containers, restoring their protection against impacts. It does not repair the cargo inside. Less effective during timefall.

## Acquisition

Unlock fabrication by completing [[missions/008_collection_chiral_crystals_distribution_center_west_of_capital_knot]]. Can also be looted from MULEs.

## Fabrication

- Chiral crystals: 16
- Resins: 8
