# Sunglasses

**Purpose:** Cosmetic eyewear. They change Sam's appearance; they do not improve vision or help detect things.

## Which should I wear?

Choose the look you prefer. The reading-style [[gordon_glasses|Gordon Glasses]] are also cosmetic.

## Sources

- [GameFAQs — Death Stranding equipment guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps5/323022-death-stranding-directors-cut/faqs/78057/equipment) — reports no apparent in-game benefit from sunglasses. The linked guide also covers other equipment.
