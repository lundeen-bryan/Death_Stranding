# Bridges Cap

**Effect:** Reduces stamina consumption while worn, helping your stamina last longer.

## How do I get it?

| Detail | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Given by | Nick Easton |
| Location | Capital Knot City |
| Unlock requirement | Reach a three-star connection with Capital Knot City |
| How to work toward it | Complete deliveries and return lost cargo addressed to Capital Knot City to earn Likes and increase its connection level |

The three-star requirement is listed in the Director's Cut guide below. This describes the unlock condition; the exact delivery that earned the cap in your playthrough has not been recorded.
