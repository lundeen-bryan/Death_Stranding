# Chiral Crystals

## Description

Hand-shaped formations of chiralium used as a material for fabrication, building and maintaining structures and roads, and fueling floating carriers.

## Acquisition

Collect from the ground in timefall-affected areas using a Crystal Collector. Defeating catchers also leaves behind large quantities of crystals. Collection is introduced in [[Missions/008_collection_chiral_crystals_distribution_center_west_of_capital_knot|Order No. 8: Collection: Chiral Crystals]].

## Fabrication

Cannot be fabricated; gathered as a raw material used to fabricate other items.
