# Gordon Glasses

**Purpose:** Cosmetic eyewear. They change Sam's appearance; they do not improve vision or help detect things.

## What are they?

The reading-style glasses modeled after Gordon Freeman's glasses from Half-Life.

Benjamin H identifies them as “Gordon Glasses” in [[lambda_you_really_are_the_great_deliverer|λ You Really Are the Great Deliverer]].

## Which should I wear?

Choose these or [[sunglasses|Sunglasses]] based on the appearance you prefer.
