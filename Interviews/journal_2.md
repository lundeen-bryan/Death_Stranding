# Journal #2

**Who:** Unknown  
**When:** Writing year unknown  
**Where:** Unknown

Another prepper's kicked the bucket. I went to deliver his medicine as usual this morning, but I guess I was pretty damn late. Poor guy wasn't just dead, but on the verge of going necro. Wasn't no time to take him to an incinerator, so I had to dump him in nearby BT territory. Better than leaving him to pop, especially since my own shelter is close by. Gotta look out for number one.

When I dumped him, though...just before he popped, it was like I could...feel the BTs reacting to the corpse. Saw the Beach, too. Strangest thing, but I'd felt it before. When I first disposed of someone. The sensation was stronger this time, though, no doubt about it. Don't know if this means I've got DOOMS, but I'm starting to think there's something...special about me.
