# Drones and the Singularity

**Who:** Mama  
**When:** Several Months Ago  
**Where:** Unknown

Yep. The bot is good to go. We're putting it through some field tests within the network service area—should have the results before long. Delivery efficiency within a given region is expected to improve following a successful Q-pid connection as well.

...No, that won't be a problem. The bot's just a cargo carrier. Human input is required to accept orders and set coordinates. Drone syndrome shouldn't be a factor, and the singularity? Not a chance.

...Well, think about it. AI doesn't die. It might get upgraded or replaced, but that's not at all comparable to death. So it has no reason to fear it, you see? The concept is completely foreign. Incomprehensible.

Death comes to all living things, but only humans have the capacity to understand the implications—to imagine what it means to no longer exist. That's one limitation AI won't ever overcome. That's the reason it won't ever surpass human intelligence.
