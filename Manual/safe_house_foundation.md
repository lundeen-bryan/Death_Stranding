# Safe House (Foundation)

The foundation of a safe house, which provides the same underground facilities as a private room. Once materials are added to complete the construction, it can be used to rest, shower, and perform Fragile jumps.

Materials required for completion:

- Chiral Crystals x 300 cg
- Special Alloys x 2400
