# The Odradek Terrain Scanner

**RB:** Use Terrain Scanner

The terrain scanner surveys the area, detecting and marking dangerous points such as steep slopes, rocks, and slippery surfaces.

Each point is marked by the following icons: **blue filled square → yellow filled square → red X**, indicating an increasing level of risk.

The terrain scanner can help find the safest route.
