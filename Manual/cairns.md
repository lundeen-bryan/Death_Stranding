# Cairns

A hologram of a cairn will appear wherever you or other players are resting. Resting near a cairn will increase the speed at which Sam recovers. The more players that rest in a given spot, the bigger the cairn will grow.

## What is a cairn?

A cairn is a pile or stack of stones, often used as a marker. It is not a mushroom. Here, the manual describes a holographic cairn marking a resting spot, where resting helps Sam recover faster.
