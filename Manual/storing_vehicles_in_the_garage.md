# Storing Vehicles in the Garage

Vehicles parked on the elevator can be stored in the garage by selecting "Garage" from the Delivery Terminal. Vehicles that have taken damage will be repaired while in the garage. Any cargo loaded on the vehicles will be transferred to a private locker. Vehicles left on the elevator while you rest in a private room will automatically be stored in the garage.
