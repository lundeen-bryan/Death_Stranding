# Vog-Preserved Cargo

Some cargo containers are filled with vog in order to preserve their contents. Vog will be emitted if the container is destroyed. Prolonged exposure to vog will cause Sam to faint, and your game to be over.

To keep your cargo containers in one piece, try to spend as little time as possible in timefall. Better yet, try to avoid it altogether.
