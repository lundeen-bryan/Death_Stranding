# Navigating List-Style Menus

| Action | Controller | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move the cursor up or down in a list-style menu | Press D-pad up or down | Press **W** or **S** |
| Change the menu category | Press D-pad left or right | Press **A** or **D** |
