# The Weather Forecast: Wind and Timefall

## Where this applies

This tip describes the **Weather overlay on the map you see when you activate the Cuff Links**. With that overlay displayed, use the blue areas and wind arrow to read the forecast.

*Context added for clarity; not part of the in-game transcription.*

## In-game text

Blue areas indicate timefall.

The arrow indicates wind speed and direction.
