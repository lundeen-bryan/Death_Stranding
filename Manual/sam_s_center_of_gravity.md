# Sam's Center of Gravity

Various factors can affect Sam's center of gravity, including how you arrange your cargo and how you move, especially if you make any sudden changes in direction. Big changes in the center of gravity will make it harder to control Sam's movement, and may ultimately result in him falling over. It's therefore important to learn how to nudge Sam's balance left and right in order to keep him on a stable footing.
