# Repairing Vehicles

Vehicles have a "durability" rating, and will no longer move once this is depleted. Durability is restored by storing a vehicle in the garage.

To store a vehicle, park it on top of the vehicle elevator.

==Container repair spray cannot be used to restore vehicles.==
