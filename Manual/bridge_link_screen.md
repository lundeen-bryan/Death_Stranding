# The Bridge Link Screen

Here you can view a list of other players you've interacted with, along with their details. You can also further strengthen your connection to other players here through the formation of Strand Contracts. Forming a Strand Contract with a player makes their structures and lost cargo easier to see.

After using **A** to select "View Own Records", use **LB** and **RB** to switch between various records.

**Strand Contracts can be formed after reaching Bridge Link Grade 10.**
