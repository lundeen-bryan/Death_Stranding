# Auto-Use and Auto-Replacement of Blood Bags

Hold **D-pad left** + **Right stick** → **A**: Equip

Any blood bags equipped with **A** will restore Sam's lost blood.

==Blood bags will be automatically removed once they are empty.==

If you have any spares, a fresh bag will automatically be equipped in place of the empty one.
