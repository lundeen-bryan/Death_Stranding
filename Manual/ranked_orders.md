# Ranked Orders

To participate in the rankings and compare your performance to that of other players, take on a ranked order or attempt a drill at the firing range when these events are available.

Your final ranking will be determined at the end of the event, and you will receive banners and medals to display on your Bridge Link Screen depending on your performance.

**The rewards you receive will depend on your ranking.**
**Your ranking will be affected by other players' results.**
