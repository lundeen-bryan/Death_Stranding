# Snow

Walking over snowy terrain is extremely hard work, and makes for very slow going indeed—simply walking through the snow is enough to gradually deplete Sam's **stamina**.

Be sure not to fall asleep while resting at high altitudes in snowy terrain—you may not wake up.
