# Floating Carriers

Floating carriers can be used to transport cargo, or as a means of personal transportation. They are kept afloat by chiral crystals, and can glide smoothly over rough terrain.

**Floating carriers will stop floating when their supply of chiral crystals has been depleted.** Two carriers can be joined together.
