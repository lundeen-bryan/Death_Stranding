# Traversing Rivers

The effort required to traverse a river is largely dependent on its depth and flow. The speed at which the Endurance Gauge is drained will be affected by the depth of the water and the strength of the current. If the gauge is completely depleted, Sam will no longer be able to resist the current, and will be swept away.

Endurance can be conserved by using both hands to center your balance as you move through the water, but simply being in the water will be enough to gradually deplete your stamina regardless.
