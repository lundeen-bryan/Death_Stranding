# Infiltrating a MULE Camp

**B:** Crouch

When infiltrating a MULE camp, crouching will help reduce the sound of your footsteps. Holding your breath will make you even harder to detect.

(If you are using a keyboard, use **Ctrl** to walk slowly, and **C** to crouch and walk.)
