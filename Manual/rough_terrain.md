# Rough Terrain

The **yellow warning triangle** icon on the scanner indicates rough terrain.

Moving or changing direction quickly on this type of terrain carries an increased risk of tripping or slipping, while jumping onto it will almost certainly result in a tumble unless you are able to quickly center your balance after landing.

This type of terrain will increase wear and tear on boots and shoes, and falling over will cause more damage to both Sam and his cargo.
