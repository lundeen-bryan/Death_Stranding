# Holding Down Buttons

**X with the hold indicator = Hold Down**

When you see the X hold icon, you will need to hold down **X**. Try holding it down until the delivery terminal responds.

Note that you will also need to hold down buttons other than X. For example, when you see the D-pad right hold icon, you will need to hold down **D-pad right**.
