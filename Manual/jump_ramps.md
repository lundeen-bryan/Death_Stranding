# Jump Ramps

Jump ramps allow those who drive over them to perform spectacular jumps. The angle of the ramp can be adjusted with **Y / A** at the point of construction.

Passing through the chiral field at the end of the ramp will give users a chiralium-powered boost to their air time, and soften the impact of landing.

Additional special actions can also be performed by those who pull off well-timed jumps.
