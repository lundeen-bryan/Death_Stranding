# Cargo Container Condition

The condition of any cargo containers you're transporting won't affect your evaluation, but as a container's condition deteriorates, its contents will become more and more susceptible to shock and impact damage. This can put cargo at serious risk when containers are dropped or rolled. As such, it's always sensible to keep your containers in good condition.
