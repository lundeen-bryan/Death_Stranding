# Timefall Farm

The Timefall Farm is located south of the [[Weather Station]] in the Central Region. The farmers use timefall to accelerate the growth of wheat and other crops.

## Unlock

Complete **Order 28: Wheat Seed Delivery to the Timefall Farmers** from the [[Weather Station]].

## Rewards

- **Order 28:** Scanner Nullification
- **3 stars:** Odradek Human Detection
- **4 stars:** Environmental Scientist hologram
- **5 stars:** Goose hologram and increased chiral bandwidth

## Useful Information

- Several time-sensitive deliveries originate here.
- Standard Order 228 to [[Lake Knot City]] is useful for raising the Delivery Time porter grade.
- Timefall Porter deliveries make beer available in private rooms.
- The MULE camp north of the farm can interfere with deliveries.

## Collectibles

- **Memory Chip 24 — Soap Bubble:** On the farm’s roof near the front railing.
- **Memory Chip 46 — Low Roar:** North of the farm.
- **Memory Chip 51 — Valhalla Rising Soundtrack:** On the mountain south of the farm.

## Director’s Cut

The racetrack is located south of the farm. It becomes available after completing Order 35 and reading Thomas Southerland’s email.
