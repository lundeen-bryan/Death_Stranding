# Wind Farm

**Region:** Eastern Region  
**Contact:** Jake Wind

## Description

A Bridges power facility northwest of the [[Locations/distribution_center_west_of_capital_knot_city|Distribution Center West of Capital Knot City]]. The approach passes over a hill and through a forest valley where BTs are a hazard.

## Details

- Supplies electricity through the chiral network once connected.
- Connecting the facility unlocks the PCC generator design.
- Provides fabrication, recycling, material claims, and private and share lockers.
- Related mission: [[Missions/009_power_supply_unit_delivery_wind_farm|Order 009 — Power Supply Unit Delivery]].
- Related mail: [[Mail/our_hard_work_is_paying_off|Our Hard Work Is Paying Off]].
