# Waystation West of Capital Knot City

**Region:** Eastern Region  
**Contact:** George Baton

## Description

A Bridges waystation between Capital Knot City and the [[Locations/distribution_center_west_of_capital_knot_city|Distribution Center West of Capital Knot City]]. Built during the first expedition, it serves as an intermediate stop for deliveries across the region.

## Details

- The first facility Sam adds to the chiral network.
- Connecting it unlocks the PCC postbox design.
- Offers fabrication, recycling, material claims, and private and share lockers.
- Nearby hazards include a MULE camp and a BT area to the south.
- Related mail: [[Mail/more_than_oxy|More than Oxy]] and [[Mail/casualties_of_twilight|Casualties of Twilight]].
