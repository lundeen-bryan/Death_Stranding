# The Engineer

**Region:** Central Region
**Contact:** The Engineer
**Location code:** S23-06

## Description

A prepper shelter occupied by the Engineer, who learned engineering from his parents and has lived in the shelter all his life. After joining the UCA, he shares equipment designs and researches evo-devo units and the odradek using information from the chiral network.
