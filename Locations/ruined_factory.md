# Ruined Factory

**Region:** Eastern Region  
**Game:** Death Stranding Director's Cut

## Description

An abandoned factory on a ridge near the [[Locations/distribution_center_west_of_capital_knot_city|Distribution Center West of Capital Knot City]]. MULEs occupy the site, where Sam is sent to recover cargo and investigate.

## Details

- Featured in the Director's Cut's additional factory orders.
- Order 077 sends Sam to retrieve data near the entrance and return it to the distribution center. A Maser Gun is supplied for the mission; completing it unlocks fabrication of the weapon.
- Related missions: [[Missions/077_collection_cargo_discovered_ruined_factory_distribution_center_w_of_capital_knot|Order 077]] and [[Missions/078_stealthy_collection_data_from_ruined_factory_distro_west_of_capital_knot|Order 078]].
