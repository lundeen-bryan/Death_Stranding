# The Craftsman

**Region**: Central Region

## Description

He's skeptical about joining the UCA so you just have to go there with a lot of hematic grenades to complete a recovery mission going to the mall area behind his shelter.

Bring 4 hematic grenades and 2 ladders. You can do it without the ladders but those can be very helpful if going over the wall.
