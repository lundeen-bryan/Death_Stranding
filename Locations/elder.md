# The Elder

**Region:** Central Region  
**Contact:** The Elder  
**Location code:** S23-18

## Description

A prepper shelter occupied by the Elder, an immigrant who lived in the United States before the Death Stranding. Initially skeptical of the UCA, he comes to trust Bridges through Sam's deliveries.
