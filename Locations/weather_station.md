# Weather Station

**Region:** Central Region  
**Contact:** Alex Weatherstone  
**Connection:** Connected through completed [[Missions/027_chiralium_gauge_delivery_weather_station|Order 027 — Chiralium Gauge Delivery: Weather Station]]. Completion reported 2026-09-19; current star rating not recorded.

## Description

A meteorological facility southwest of [[Locations/distribution_center_south_of_lake_knot_city|Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City]]. Before joining the network, its small team could gather weather data only locally and could not share it widely; atmospheric chiralium prevented access to weather satellites.

## Details

- Connecting the station enables the weather forecast feature, useful for planning around timefall.
- The approach from the distribution center includes rivers and rocky terrain with BTs.
- The initial delivery unlocks PCC Lv. 2, Timefall Shelters, Safe Houses, and Floating Carrier Lv. 1. See the mission note for the full list.
