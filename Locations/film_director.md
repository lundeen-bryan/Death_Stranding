# Film Director

**Region:** Central Region  
**Contact:** The Film Director  
**Location code:** S23-71

## Description

A prepper shelter northeast of the Junk Dealer. Its resident grew up after the Death Stranding, studying surviving films and imagery. He values cinema and the shared experience of watching movies in theaters.

## First delivery and sunglasses

Accept **Order 031 — Tablet Delivery: Film Director** at [[Locations/distribution_center_south_of_lake_knot_city|Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City]]. Completing it connects his shelter to the chiral network and rewards **"Sam" sunglasses**. He joins the UCA at two stars.

The sunglasses are an order reward; you do not need to reach five stars to receive them.
