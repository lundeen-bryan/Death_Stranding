# Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City

**Region:** Central Region  
**Contact:** Thomas Southerland  
**Location code:** UCA-23-086

## Description

A Bridges distribution center beside a river and along Route 23, which connects Lake Knot City to South Knot City. It serves nearby preppers, though two nearby MULE camps make deliveries hazardous.

## Details

- Has a private room, garage, and firing range.
- Connecting the facility unlocks bot-deliverable standard orders and Bridges Boots Lv. 2.
- Backpack customization and the basic Truck are unlocked through Order 035, rather than a connection level.

## Connection Rewards

| Connection level | Notable rewards |
| --- | --- |
| 3 stars | Vehicle colors; increased material storage and chiral bandwidth |
| 4 stars | Truck: Long Range Lv. 2; Thomas Southerland hologram; additional vehicle colors |
| 5 stars | Truck: Long Range Lv. 3; additional vehicle colors; Bridges General Staff patch in Director's Cut |
