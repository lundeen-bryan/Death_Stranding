# Port Knot City

**Region:** Eastern Region  
**Contact:** Viktor Frank

## Description

A port city on the crater lake at Ground Zero. It connects the Eastern Region with the Central Region through the shipping route to Lake Knot City, operated by Fragile Express.

## Details

- Contains a Bridges distribution center, private room, and garage.
- Offers fabrication, recycling, material claims, and a share locker once connected.
- Sam brings an aid shipment here to connect the city to the chiral network.
