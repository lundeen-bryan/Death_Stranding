## My recommended route — on foot

I found the easiest way to do this mission is **on foot through the crevices below where the MULEs patrol**. This sets off their alarm, but in my experience, if I keep moving they do not catch me.

At the end of the crevice heading toward the Cosplayer, I can **walk up a hill to get out**.
