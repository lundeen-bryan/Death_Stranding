# Standard Order 432 - Material Collection & Delivery

**Accept at:** [[Locations/lake_knot_city|Lake Knot City]]
**Collect from:** Lake Knot City harbor / port
**Deliver to:** [[Locations/lake_knot_city|Lake Knot City]] delivery terminal
**Cargo:** Materials for collection and delivery
**Full load:** 288.0 kg in 9 extra-large containers (XL-9)
**Evaluation category:** Miscellaneous

## Mission

- [ ] Accept Standard Order 432 at Lake Knot City's terminal.
- [ ] Collect the materials from the harbor and return them to Lake Knot City's delivery terminal.
- [ ] Record the result, Likes, and any materials added to the facility stores.

The community order list gives a minimum of **1 container for the standard order** or **6 containers for premium delivery**. The complete available load is 9 containers. Check the terminal's current requirements before accepting.

## Preparation

Plan for the full 288 kg load. A published player account completed the order using **two floating carriers**; these provide a documented transport option for this collection trip.

The order tracker lists approximately **292 / 410 Likes** for standard / premium delivery. Actual rewards vary: the published player account reports 519 Likes. These figures are reference results, not the player's recorded progress.

## Rewards and progress

Acceptance and completion have not been reported. The sources checked identify a materials collection order and do not identify a vehicle blueprint unlock for Order 432. Specific material types and amounts added to facility stores are not confirmed here; record them from the order and results screens.
