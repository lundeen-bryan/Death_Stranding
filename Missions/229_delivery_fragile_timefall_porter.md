## Order details and clarification

The published standard-order list gives the route as **Timefall Farm → Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City**. The farm is the pickup location, not the delivery destination. The cargo is 30 kg of Timefall Porter, and the evaluation focuses on cargo condition.

Redeploy Cargo keeps the order active and returns the cargo to its pickup location. It does not complete or skip the order. Canceling is a separate action.
