# Standard Order 410 - Delivery: Old Board Game — "The Big Delivery"

**From:** The Collector ([[Locations/the_collector]])
**To:** [[Locations/distribution_center_south_of_lake_knot_city|Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City]]

**Containers:** 1 small container (S-1)
**Evaluation category:** Cargo Condition

## Mission

- [ ] Check the Collector's terminal for Standard Order 410, accept it, and deliver the board game to Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City.
- [ ] Check the delivery rewards and fabrication menu for a Long Range Truck unlock; record the truck level received.

The community order list gives damage limits of **under 50% for the standard order** and **under 20% for premium delivery**, with approximately **15 / 17 Likes** respectively. These are listed estimates, not the player's actual results. Check the terminal for the current requirements.

## Long Range Truck unlock

**Player finding, 2026-10-01:** Standard Order 410 unlocks the Long Range Truck. Completion and the specific truck level have not yet been reported.

**Online research:** PowerPyx identifies Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City's facility progression as the source of multiple Long Range Truck levels. The order list confirms that Order 410 delivers to this center, but does not list a truck as a unique reward for this order.

**Interpretation:** Completing 410 may trigger the truck blueprint through the center's connection-level progression. The sources checked do not establish that Order 410 is a mandatory, exclusive unlock requirement. Record the reward screen and connection level when completing it.
