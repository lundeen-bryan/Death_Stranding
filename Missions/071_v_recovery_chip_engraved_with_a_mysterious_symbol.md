# Order 071 - [V] Recovery: Chip Engraved with a Mysterious Symbol

**From:** [[Locations/distribution_center_south_of_lake_knot_city|Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City]] — Thomas Southerland  
**To:** [[Locations/distribution_center_south_of_lake_knot_city|Distribution Center South of Lake Knot City]]  
**Cargo:** V-marked data chip  
**Weight:** Not verified  
**Distance:** Not recorded  
**Transport:** Not recorded

## Mission

Accept the Cyberpunk 2077 collaboration recovery order at the distribution center, retrieve the chip to its south, and return it. The guide clarifies that the recovery site is relatively close to the distribution center despite the mail's mention of a crater lake near South Knot City.

Triggering mail: [[Mail/recovery_request_v_track_down_a_chip_with_a_weird_symbol_on_it|[Recovery Request] [V] Track Down a Chip with a Weird Symbol on It]].

## Documented unlocks

- **V's Face Pattern.**
- **Compass upgrade:** option to display the objective marker at all times.

These are guide-listed unlocks for this order. The sensor-pole hacking upgrade and Yaiba Kusanagi trike belong to the subsequent Order 072, not this recovery.
